I scanned / for regular files with SUID or world-writable permission bits.

Results:
- 63 SUID paths
- 189,495 world-writable paths
- 188,609 world-writable paths under /mnt/c
- 886 world-writable paths outside /mnt/c

The scan returned exit status 1 and recorded 397 error lines.
Of these, 394 referenced /mnt/c. The other three reported a
filesystem loop at /mnt/wslg/distro and two unavailable /proc paths.

The outside-C results include virtual /proc files and WSLg logs.
The inventories reflect permission bits reported through WSL.
Inaccessible paths could not be fully enumerated, so these results
do not establish complete coverage of every file under /.
The original results and error log were retained.
